# Default humanoid

The Axcenity default humanoid: a smooth, stylized person, 1.75 m tall, in a relaxed A-pose. The default outfit is a cropped navy jacket with ribbed cuffs and hem and a light band round each upper arm, over a white T-shirt, with black cargo joggers (side pockets, gathered cuffs) and white sneakers with navy toe caps and heel tabs. The default hair is short at the back and sides with the top swept over from a side part. There's no logo. The art direction, the assessment of the old faceted character and the decisions are in [docs/research/character-studio.md](../../../docs/research/character-studio.md). The skeleton and animation contract are in [docs/research/default-humanoid.md](../../../docs/research/default-humanoid.md).

The body is built on Blender Studio's **Human Base Meshes** (stylized male and female, CC0 1.0), vendored in `source/`. Everything else is generated by `build.py`: re-posing, skeleton fitting, weights, every shape, the face's parts, the clothes, the hair, the materials and textures. To change the character, change the scripts and run them again.

| File | What it is |

## How it's built

- **Two frames, one topology.** The bundle's stylized male and female bodies share their vertices one for one. The masculine body is the mesh; the feminine one is its `Frame_Feminine` shape. Both are stood at 1.75 m.
- **Re-posed.** The base bodies hang their arms about 60° below level; the build fits a skeleton, weights a copy, turns the arms up to 45° and keeps the result (twice, so the final fit lands on 45°). The resting eyelids come down a little, so the upper lid just covers the top of the iris.
- **The skeleton from face sets.** The bundle's sculpt face sets mark every region, down to each finger joint. Where two sets meet is a ring round the limb, and its centre is the joint. The same rings fit both frames; the difference is each bone's `offset` for `Frame_Feminine`.
- **Weights.** Blender's bone-heat weights from the fitted skeleton, the jaw's by hand (the lower lip, chin, mouth floor and under the jaw, smoothed so the cheeks stretch), at most four per vertex.
- **The face.** Eyelids turn about the eyeball's centre, so blinks slide over the eye. The mouth is the base mesh's own mouth bag, opened by the `Jaw` bone. Brows and lashes are generated geometry that follows the skin (lashes turn with their lid). The teeth are the bundle's stylized jaw, sized into the mouth (upper on `Head`, lower on `Jaw`).
- **Clothes** start from the body's own surface where they're worn, are cut with planes at the hems (a sleeve's plane cuts only the arm), then pushed out and draped: smoothed while never closer to the skin than their gap, so cloth bridges hollows. Colour bands are cut as edge loops. Every garment follows the body's frame and build shapes and its weights.
- **Clearance** (`fit.py`), in the rest pose, in each shape and in the feminine frame mixed with each build: every garment is pushed clear of the body with its masks on; where a garment is worn under another (the T-shirt under the jacket, the trousers' waistband under the untucked T-shirt, the sports top under the jacket) the inner one takes the outer one's shape deltas, so the two move together, and is tucked 3 mm under it. A sneaker's sole encloses the upper's bottom and the foot's own footprint.
- **Hair** is a cap over the scalp plus clumps grown along the style's comb direction, merged into one sculpted mass by a voxel remesh, relaxed and decimated to the style's budget.
- **Skin** colour is a 2048 px texture baked from a colour field: plain tone, warmer lips, cheeks, nose, ears, knuckles, elbows and knees. The base mesh's four UDIM tiles are packed into the quarters of one texture.

## Rebuilding

The scripts need Blender 4.2 or later (written against 5.3). Either:

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup --python assets/characters/humanoid/build.py -- --out assets/characters/humanoid
```

or, without Blender installed, with the `bpy` module from PyPI (Python 3.13 for bpy 5.x):

```bash
python -m venv .bpy && .bpy/bin/pip install bpy
.bpy/bin/python assets/characters/humanoid/build.py --out assets/characters/humanoid
```

It takes about 15 seconds and prints each part's triangle count. Copy `Humanoid.glb` and `HumanoidAnimations.glb` into `examples/hello-humanoid/assets/models/Humanoid/` too. Refreshing `source/` from the bundle: `python source/extract.py path/to/human_base_meshes_bundle.blend` (see the URL and checksum in `extract.py`).

## Conventions

- **Units and axes:** metres; Blender Z up with the character facing -Y, so the glTF files are Y up facing +Z (glTF's front). Feet on the ground at the origin. The engine's character controller faces -Z, so the default character's MeshPart turns it 180° (`Orientation` Y 180). Placed in the engine at 5 studs tall (1 stud = 0.35 m).
- **Skeleton:** unchanged from the previous character: Unity and VRM humanoid bone names, which the engine's retargeting reads as roles with no rig definition: `Root` (on the ground, for root motion), `Hips`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `UpperChest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Jaw`, `LeftEye`/`RightEye`, and per side `Shoulder`, `UpperArm`, `LowerArm`, `Hand`, three bones for each finger and the thumb (`LeftIndexProximal`, `…Intermediate`, `…Distal`), `UpperLeg`, `LowerLeg`, `Foot`, `Toes`.
- **Bone axes** (for keying by hand): +X swings a limb forward, bends an elbow and leans the spine forward; a knee bends with -X; +X on a foot lifts the toes; +Y on the spine or head turns toward the character's left; finger curl is -X. Right-side angles mirror the left (Y and Z negated).
- **Materials** (each its own glTF material, so a tint can find one by name): `Skin` (textured), `Mouth`, `Teeth`, `Gums`, `Sclera`, `Iris` (textured, tintable), `Brow`, `Lash`, `Hair`, `HairSecondary` (the clumps' ends), `Shirt`, `Jacket`, `JacketTrim`, `JacketRib`, `Trousers`, `Shoes`, `ShoeAccent`, `Sole`, `Laces`, `Underwear`.
- **Shapes on the body** (at weight 0 unless a file says otherwise):
  - The 41 expression shapes, named as the Axcenity facial profile's controls: `Blink_*`, `Eye_Closed_*`, `Eye_Squint_*`, `Eye_Wide_*`, `Brow_Raise_*`, `Brow_Lower_*`, `Brow_Inward_*`, `Mouth_Smile_*`, `Mouth_Frown_*`, `Mouth_Open`, `Mouth_Wide`, `Mouth_Narrow`, `Mouth_Pucker`, `Mouth_Left`, `Mouth_Right`, `Upper_Lip_Raise` (and `_Left/_Right`), `Lower_Lip_Lower` (and `_Left/_Right`), `Lip_Corner_Pull_*`, `Cheek_Raise_*`, `Cheek_Puff`, `Nose_Sneer_*`, `Jaw_Open` (a corrective for the `Jaw` bone's turn), `Jaw_Forward`, `Jaw_Left`, `Jaw_Right`. Brows and lashes carry the same shapes.
  - The frame and build: `Frame_Feminine`, `Build_Heavy`, `Build_Slim`, `Build_Muscular`. Garments and hair carry the ones that change them.
  - Face identity (Character Studio's face sliders): `Face_Eyes_Spacing`, `Face_Eyes_Height`, `Face_Eyes_Size`, `Face_Eyes_Tilt`, `Face_Brows_Height`, `Face_Nose_Width`, `Face_Nose_Length`, `Face_Nose_Bridge`, `Face_Nose_Tip`, `Face_Mouth_Width`, `Face_Lips_Full`, `Face_Chin_Length`, `Face_Chin_Forward`, `Face_Jaw_Width`, `Face_Cheekbones`, `Face_Head_Width`, `Face_Head_Length`, `Face_Ears_Size`, `Face_Ears_Out`. Those that move the eyes list the eye bones' change in the customization file.
  - Clothing masks: `Mask_Torso`, `Mask_Hips`, `Mask_UpperArms_Top`, `Mask_UpperArms_Low`, `Mask_Forearms`, `Mask_Thighs_Top`, `Mask_Thighs_Low`, `Mask_Shins_Top`, `Mask_Shins_Low`, `Mask_Feet`. At weight 1 a mask pulls its skin about a centimetre in (0.8 cm at the feet), feathering where the next bone's weights take over.
- **Animations** (in place unless named `_RootMotion`, where `Root` travels): Idle, Walk, Run, Sprint, WalkBackward, WalkLeft, WalkRight, RunBackward, RunLeft, RunRight, Jump, Fall, Land, Crouch, CrouchWalk, Wave, Point, PickUp, Use, Push, Pull, Interact, Swim, Climb, Sit, Cheer, Clap, Shrug, Dance, HitReact, Walk_RootMotion, Run_RootMotion.
- **Export settings that matter:** sparse shape keys (`export_try_sparse_sk=True`; the engine reads Blender's empty sparse accessors as no moves), no shape-key normals (they doubled the files).

## Known limitations

- `Frame_Feminine` and the eye identity shapes move bones as well as vertices. Until Character Studio's appearance applies the bone offsets (CS3), leaning a file's shape by hand moves the face but not the eyeballs.
- Hair is sculpted masses with clump grooves, not strands; there's no curly style yet (curls smooth away in the remesh).
- No fabric or skin normal maps yet: folds, cuffs and pockets are geometry; finer seams and weave aren't shown.
