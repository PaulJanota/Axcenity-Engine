# `procedural`

- Phase 74: what a SplineMesh (`bend.rs`: a model bent along the curve, repeated to fill it) and a SplineScatter (`scatter.rs`, standing on `surface.rs`'s probe) make from their Spline, kept up to date by `Generators::update` (`lib.rs`), and Flatten Terrain Along (`flatten.rs`). Behavior: `docs/architecture/splines.md`.
- Results are the generator's children, never saved, sent or kept in slots (`core::spline::is_generator`); their bent models are `:runtime/spline<N>.glb` in `Assets`, forgotten when replaced.
- Deterministic: the same spline, settings and seed give the same layout. Use `rng.rs` (per spot, from the seed and the spot's number), never `rand`, and never iterate a `HashMap` into output. Trees (`tree/`, foliage T1–T3, plan `docs/research/foliage-grass-seasons.md`; Insert ▸ Tree `docs/editor/building.md`): `.axtree` recipes (`recipe.rs`, maps in `assets/trees/textures`) grown into limbs (`skeleton.rs`), bark with a finer `TrunkBase` and simpler copies within triangle budgets (`mesher.rs`) and shade-thinned leaf clusters, cut-out cards when textured, with crown data (`leaves.rs`), and every corner's packed colour (`paint.rs`, `COLOR_0`); clean-room (no Mtree or SpeedTree code); bump `TREE_GENERATOR_VERSION` when output changes.
- `terrain_scatter.rs` (PW1): ScatterRules inside a Terrain placed per 32-sample chunk into `TerrainData::set_generated`, never saved; plan `docs/research/procedural-worlds.md`, behavior `docs/terrain.md`.
- PW4: `paths.rs` (TerrainPath settings, the keep-off strips `terrain_scatter` reads live and thins past, `apply_path` with its shoulder layer, ragged edge and painted-foliage clearing; thinning is by a hash of each spot's place, never a sequence) and `route.rs` (A* over the heightfield within a grade, the road's height in the search). Behavior: `docs/architecture/splines.md` (Terrain paths).
- PW5: `obstacles.rs` (anchored parts rules keep off, `Obstacles` cached by revision; `Obstacle::part_box` for any part) and `footprint.rs` (Blend into Terrain). PW6: `building.rs` (BuildingGenerator's footprint, bays, wedge-pair slabs and roofs; made by `make_building` in `lib.rs`; behavior `docs/architecture/splines.md`, Buildings).
- Depends on `core`, `assets` and `terrain` only, so the server and the player share it (physics only in tests). `decal_scatter.rs` is only the layout of one stamp of the editor's decal brush (spacing, the renderer's decal budget; `docs/editor/scatter.md`); the editor makes the Instances.
