# Wind and swaying plants

Plants bend in the wind in the vertex shader (graphics Phase 28). The wind is the Workspace's `GlobalWind` (studs a second), the same wind that blows particles, rain and snow and carries the clouds. Nothing about it is saved apart from `GlobalWind` and a material file's `sway`.

## What sways

- **Built-in plants** painted on terrain (`scene/src/foliage.rs`): grass and flowers give most (strength 1 and 0.8), bushes less (0.35), trees least (0.2, the prop tree too). Other props stay still. A plant's pieces (a tree's trunk and crown) share one `Sway`, from its ground to the top of its highest piece, so they bend as one.
- **Parts and MeshParts with a material file** whose `sway` is above 0 (`docs/rendering/models.md`): they sway by height from the bottom of the part's box to its top, with that strength. The Material tab has a **Sway** slider (0 to 2). Instances inherit it from their base like other values.
- **Models that say so**: a glTF material's `extras` can hold `axcenity_sway` (0 to 2, as a material file's `sway`) and `axcenity_flutter` (studs, up to 0.95). They work on a model's own materials, painted on a terrain or on a MeshPart; a MeshPart's `MaterialVariant` replaces them with its file's `sway`. Every piece of the model bends by height over the bottom of the model's box, so give a tree's bark and leaves the same `axcenity_sway` and only the leaves an `axcenity_flutter`. A flutter with no sway (0 or left out) shivers the leaves without leaning anything: ivy on a wall, whose wall must stay put (2026-11-26; before, a flutter without a sway was ignored). Files without these keys stay still, and older copies of the engine ignore them.
- **What hangs** (drapes, washing on a line): a glTF material whose `extras` have `axcenity_hang` (`true` or a number above 0) beside its `axcenity_sway` sways from the top of its model's box down: its top stays put and its foot swings downwind and rises a little, as a pendulum does (`assets::ModelMaterial::hangs`, `render::Sway::hanging`: a negative `Sway::height`, with `base` where it hangs from). Make each hanging thing a model of its own, since it sways by its own box.
- **Per corner** (foliage W3b, ADR-0053): a glTF material whose `extras` have `axcenity_foliage: true` says its `COLOR_0` is a plant's packed data, not colours (`assets::ModelMaterial::foliage_data`; a generated tree writes it, `procedural`'s `tree/paint.rs`). `G` (0 to 1) is that corner's share of the lean, in place of its height's: a tree's trunk stays stiff while its limbs give more toward their tips and each leaf takes its twig's. `B` (0 to 1) is its share of the material's flutter (thin twigs, leaf tips and edges shiver most). `R` and `A` shade it (below). Without the key a file's vertex colours are left alone and it sways by height.
- FBX materials don't sway.

## How

`render::DrawItem::sway` (`Sway { base, height, strength }`, `None` to keep still) goes into the item's instance data (`InstanceRaw::sway`, location 15), and `DrawList::wind` goes into the frame uniform's spare `clock.yzw`. `swayed` in shader.wgsl moves each corner in world space after skinning:

- Its weight is its height over `base` as a share of `height`, squared, so the foot stays put and the top moves most. A negative `height` (what hangs) measures down from `base`, so the top stays put and the foot moves most; its lean uses the height's size, and the "drop" below then lifts the foot.
- It leans downwind along the wind's level part, at most `SWAY_LEAN` (a quarter) of its height times its strength, reaching about two thirds of that at 20 studs a second (`1 − e^(−speed / SWAY_WIND)`). A vertical wind doesn't bend plants.
- Gusts (`cloud_noise2` over the ground, rolling with the wind) vary the lean between 0.7 and 1.2 of it, and a flutter (a sine whose phase varies smoothly over the ground) adds ±0.15. Both vary smoothly with world position, so pieces that touch stay together.
- The top drops a little as it bends, so a plant keeps its length.
- A flutter (`Sway::flutter`, in the fraction of `InstanceRaw::sway.w`, whose whole part is the decal role) shivers each corner by that many studs, across and along the wind and a little upward, in quick waves whose phase comes from where the corner rests, so neighbouring leaves don't match. Gusts drive it, and the bottom quarter of the plant fades to none.
- With no wind (or a NaN one), nothing moves: the frame is exactly the same as with no sway.

The scene and prepass pipelines (`vs_main`) and the shadow pipeline (`vs_shadow`) call the same function (`swayed_by`, in sway.wgsl; `swayed_at` is it with no weights of its own), so shadows and ambient occlusion move with the plant.

**Weights per corner.** A mesh with `MeshSource::foliage` keeps it on the GPU as one word a corner, 8 bits a value (`renderer/foliage.rs`). Each frame the meshes some view draws are copied into one table (the frame's binding 20, also in the shadow passes' group; compute layouts leave it out), kept while they fit and filled afresh with just the frame's meshes when they don't, and each unposed instance and caster drawn with one gets `skin = [its first corner there, FOLIAGE]` (`FOLIAGE_CORNERS` in shader.wgsl, never a posed item's 1 or one waiting to be posed). `corner_foliage` reads a corner's word by `vertex_index` in `vs_main`, `vs_shadow`, `vs_shadow_cut_out` and `vs_motion` (last frame's bend uses the same weights); `pose_corner`, `previous_corner` and the snow slab treat `FOLIAGE` as at rest, and a lamp's kept shadow doesn't count it as moving. The ray traced scene's bending binds the mesh's own buffer (`ray_sway.wgsl`, binding 4), so its bent copies match. The weights never go past 1, so `Sway::reach` still bounds every corner. In the fragment shader `R` (how open the corner is to the sky) multiplies the occlusion, and for a leaf it stands in for `lit_leaf`'s guess at the crown from the item's box; `A` is a leaf's cluster shade (`leaf_tone`, in place of the tone that varies by position) and bark's weathering, which turns it toward pale silver-grey (`weathered_wood`). Ray traced shadows, AO, GI and the fog's traced shafts move with it too: the ray traced scene bends swaying items' meshes with the same function into BLASes they share each frame, nearest first within a budget, leaving out those the wind moves less than a pixel on screen ([ray-tracing.md](ray-tracing.md)). The shadow passes bind the frame uniform for it (binding 0 of their group). Culling grows a swaying item's bounding sphere by `Sway::reach`, the farthest any corner can move, so a plant just out of view that can lean into it is still drawn. Normals aren't bent.

Time is `DrawList::time`. The editor keeps drawing frames while the wind blows something that sways (`viewport::shader_time`).

## Cost

One more instance attribute per item (16 bytes) and a few instructions per corner for items that sway. No pass, target or draw call is added. Items that don't sway skip it on one test.

Measured on Temperate Dry Patch at Ultra 2560 × 1440 (`profile_project`'s `no-wind` and `no-sway`, three cameras; [performance.md](performance.md)): the wind costs 0.01 to 0.14 ms of a 9 ms GPU frame, inside the run-to-run noise. There's nothing per plant on the CPU and nothing updated per object: one `GlobalWind` goes into the frame uniform, and the vertex shader works out each corner's lean, gust and flutter from its world position and the time. Plants out of view, or past their cull distance, aren't drawn, so they cost nothing at all; shadows sway in the shadow pass from the same function, so a plant coming into view or into a cascade is already where it should be. Distance-based wind quality, slower updates far away and visibility-aware simulation were considered and not built, since there's no simulation to schedule and the shader's share of the frame can't be measured apart from noise.

## Tests

- `render/src/renderer/tests/foliage_data.rs` (GPU): a plant whose corners' sway weights are 0 stays put in a gale, with weights of 1 its foot moves as far as its top, and half the weight leans it about half as far; its shadow follows the weights in the shadow maps and in ray traced shadows (ray queries only); its baked occlusion darkens bark and leaves, open data with no mask lights bark as none does, weathering makes bark paler and greyer, and a leaf's mask moves its tone. `renderer/foliage.rs`: the packing, and ranges across instance pieces. `scene`: only a material marked `foliage_data` hands its colours on (`ModelMesh::foliage`).
- `render/src/renderer/tests/wind.rs` (GPU): with ray traced shadows and no shadow maps, the plant's shadow moves with it in a gale, one item is bent, and none without wind (`a_swaying_plants_ray_traced_shadow_moves_with_it`, ray queries only); three plants of two meshes bent together each cast a shadow that moves with them, each still an item of the ray traced scene, and a plant whose sway is under a pixel isn't bent (`swaying_plants_bent_together_each_cast_their_own_moving_shadow`, `a_plant_whose_sway_is_under_a_pixel_is_traced_at_rest`); a plant leans toward +X in a wind to +X and toward −X in one to −X, less in a breeze than in a gale, not sideways in a wind toward the camera, and its foot stays put. With no wind, or with no sway, the frame is exactly as still. Its shadow's end moves as many studs as its top does (within 35%; about 1.06 studs each in a 40-stud-a-second wind). A swaying plant just out of view isn't culled where a still one is.
- `scene/src/foliage.rs`: a tree's trunk and crown share one sway from its ground to its top, grass gives more than a tree, and crates stay still.
- `scene/src/material.rs`: a material file's `sway` sways a part from the bottom of its box by its height, one without doesn't, and `GlobalWind` reaches `DrawList::wind`.
- `assets/src/material.rs`: `sway` round-trips and an instance takes its base's.
- `assets/src/model/tests.rs`: a glTF material's `extras` give its sway and flutter, kept within their limits, and whether it hangs (one value of the wrong sort doesn't lose the others).
- `render/src/renderer/tests/wind.rs`: what hangs swings its foot downwind (either way) while where it hangs from stays put, and is still without wind. `scene/src/material.rs`: a hanging model's sway starts at the top of its box.
- `scene/src/material.rs`: a model's own material sways it from the bottom of its box. `render/src/renderer/tests/wind.rs`: leaves flutter at the top of a plant and not at its foot, and with no sway at all (`leaves_flutter_with_no_sway_at_all`; `scene`'s `leaves_that_only_flutter_still_move`).
